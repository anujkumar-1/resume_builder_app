📖 My story · Why I built this

Hey! I'm Anuj, a software engineer based in New Delhi. I love building cool stuff — from web apps to developer tools. But like many of you, I got extremely frustrated with online resume builders.

“Spend 15 minutes carefully crafting your resume, only to hit a paywall at the download step. No thanks.”

That frustration turned into action. I built MyJobCV — a completely free, no-paywall, no-surprise resume builder. You invest your time, you get a beautiful, professional resume. No credit card, no hidden fees. Just honest tools for job seekers.

🧠 My mission: empower everyone to create a standout resume without being tricked into subscriptions. Every feature is 100% free. Period.
